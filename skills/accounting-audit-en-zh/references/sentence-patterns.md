# 财务报表高频句式中英对照（155 组）

按「英文 → 中文」成对使用。左边是英文年报/教材里真实出现的句式，右边是可直接嵌入译文的中文表述。
背法是**整句背**：句式比单词更值钱，句式记住了，换主语换数字就能用。


## Part 1 会计原理

### 第 1 章 Accounting and Its Environment / 会计与环境

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Accounting has been called “the language of business”. | 会计被称为“商业语言”。 |
| Its function is to provide quantitative information, primarily financial in nature, about economic entities that is intended to be useful in making economic decisions. | 其职能是提供关于经济主体的、以财务性质为主的数量信息，以便用于经济决策。 |
| The accounting process is identifying, measuring, recording, and communicating economic information to permit informed judgments and decisions by users of information. | 会计程序即识别、计量、记录并传递经济信息，使信息使用者能够做出有依据的判断与决策。 |
| Users of accounting information are divided into two major categories: internal and external. | 会计信息使用者分为两大类：内部使用者与外部使用者。 |
| The going-concern assumption reflects accountants’ belief that a business will continue to operate long enough to use its longest-lived asset. | 持续经营假设反映会计人员的信念：企业将持续经营足够长的时间，以至于能使用完其寿命最长的资产。 |
| The accrual basis recognizes that a company’s financial position and performance can change without any cash changing hands. | 权责发生制承认：企业的财务状况与经营成果可能在没有任何现金易手的情况下发生变动。 |
| A business transaction is an event which affects the financial position of an entity and can be reliably measured and recorded. | 交易事项是指影响主体财务状况、且能够可靠计量与记录的事件。 |
| The cash basis is not consistent with the IASB framework. | 收付实现制与 IASB 框架不一致。 |

### 第 2 章 Accounting Concepts and Principles / 会计概念与原则

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Relevance and faithful representation are the two fundamental qualitative characteristics of useful financial information. | 相关性与忠实反映是有用财务信息的两项基本质量特征。 |
| Information is material if omitting or misstating it could influence decisions that users make. | 如果遗漏或错报某项信息会影响使用者的决策，该信息就是重要的。 |
| An asset is a present economic resource controlled by the entity as a result of past events. | 资产是由过去事项形成、由主体控制的现时经济资源。 |
| A liability is a present obligation of the entity to transfer an economic resource as a result of past events. | 负债是由过去事项形成的、主体转移经济资源的现时义务。 |
| Equity is the residual interest in the assets of the entity after deducting all its liabilities. | 权益是主体资产扣除全部负债后的剩余利益。 |
| Recognition is the process of capturing for inclusion in the financial statements an item that meets the definition of an element. | 确认是将符合要素定义的项目纳入财务报表的过程。 |
| Prudence is the exercise of caution when making judgements under conditions of uncertainty. | 谨慎性是在不确定条件下做出判断时保持审慎。 |
| The cost of providing information must not exceed the benefits derived from it. | 提供信息的成本不应超过由此获得的收益。 |

### 第 3 章 Financial Statements / 财务报表

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| The statement of financial position reports the assets, liabilities and equity of an entity at a specific date. | 财务状况表报告主体在特定日期的资产、负债与权益。 |
| Assets are classified as current and non-current according to the operating cycle. | 资产按营业周期划分为流动与非流动。 |
| Gross profit is revenue less cost of sales. | 毛利等于收入减去销售成本。 |
| Total comprehensive income comprises profit or loss and other comprehensive income. | 综合收益总额由当期损益与其他综合收益构成。 |
| Revenue is recognised when control of the goods or services transfers to the customer. | 当商品或服务的控制权转移给客户时确认收入。 |
| The statement of cash flows classifies cash flows into operating, investing and financing activities. | 现金流量表把现金流量分为经营、投资与筹资三类活动。 |
| Depreciation is added back in the indirect method because it is a non-cash expense. | 间接法下折旧被加回，因为它是非现金费用。 |
| The statement of changes in equity links profit for the year to closing equity. | 权益变动表把本年利润与期末权益衔接起来。 |

### 第 4 章 The Accounting Cycle / 会计循环

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Every transaction affects at least two accounts and keeps the accounting equation in balance. | 每笔交易至少影响两个账户，并使会计等式保持平衡。 |
| Debits must equal credits for every journal entry. | 每笔分录的借方合计必须等于贷方合计。 |
| Adjusting entries are made at the end of the period to match revenues and expenses. | 期末编制调整分录，以实现收入与费用的配比。 |
| An accrued expense is an expense incurred but not yet paid or recorded. | 应计费用是已经发生但尚未支付或记录的费用。 |
| Unearned revenue is a liability because the entity still owes goods or services to the customer. | 预收收入是负债，因为主体仍欠客户商品或服务。 |
| Closing entries transfer the balances of temporary accounts to retained earnings. | 结账分录把临时性账户余额转入留存收益。 |
| A trial balance lists all account balances to check that debits equal credits. | 试算平衡表列示全部账户余额，以检验借贷是否相等。 |
| A trial balance does not guarantee that no errors were made. | 试算平衡并不能保证没有发生错误。 |


## Part 2 财务会计

### 第 5 章 Current Assets / 流动资产

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Cash equivalents are short-term, highly liquid investments that are readily convertible to known amounts of cash. | 现金等价物是期限短、流动性强、易于转换为已知金额现金的投资。 |
| Under the allowance method, bad debt expense is estimated and matched with the related sales. | 备抵法下，坏账费用被估计并与相关销售收入配比。 |
| Inventory is measured at the lower of cost and net realisable value. | 存货按成本与可变现净值孰低计量。 |
| FIFO assumes that the items of inventory purchased first are sold first. | 先进先出法假定先购入的存货先售出。 |
| The weighted average method assigns the same unit cost to all units of inventory. | 加权平均法对所有存货单位分配相同的单位成本。 |
| A write-down is recognised when the net realisable value falls below cost. | 当可变现净值低于成本时确认存货跌价。 |
| Separation of duties reduces the risk of fraud over cash. | 职责分离降低了现金舞弊的风险。 |
| Auditors attend the physical inventory count to obtain evidence about its existence. | 审计人员参加存货实地盘点，以获取存货存在性的证据。 |

### 第 6 章 Investments, Plant Assets and Intangibles / 投资、固定资产与无形资产

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Plant assets are initially measured at cost, including all expenditure directly attributable to bringing the asset to working condition. | 固定资产按成本进行初始计量，成本包括使资产达到可使用状态所发生的直接可归属支出。 |
| Depreciation is the systematic allocation of the depreciable amount of an asset over its useful life. | 折旧是在资产使用寿命内对可折旧金额进行的系统分配。 |
| Under the revaluation model, an increase in value is recognised in other comprehensive income. | 重估模式下，价值增加计入其他综合收益。 |
| An impairment loss is recognised when the carrying amount exceeds the recoverable amount. | 当账面价值超过可收回金额时确认减值损失。 |
| Research expenditure is expensed as incurred, whereas development expenditure may be capitalised. | 研究支出在发生时费用化，开发支出则可能资本化。 |
| Goodwill is not amortised but is tested for impairment annually. | 商誉不摊销，但每年进行减值测试。 |
| A gain or loss on disposal is the difference between the proceeds and the carrying amount. | 处置损益是处置价款与账面价值之差。 |
| Capital expenditure is added to the asset, while revenue expenditure is expensed. | 资本性支出计入资产，收益性支出计入当期费用。 |

### 第 7 章 Liabilities / 负债

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| A liability is a present obligation arising from past events, the settlement of which is expected to result in an outflow of economic benefits. | 负债是由过去事项形成的现时义务，其清偿预期会导致经济利益流出。 |
| A provision is recognised when an outflow is probable and can be reliably estimated. | 当经济利益很可能流出且金额能可靠估计时确认预计负债。 |
| A contingent liability is disclosed but not recognised. | 或有负债只披露、不确认。 |
| Bonds are issued at a discount when the coupon rate is lower than the market rate. | 当票面利率低于市场利率时，债券折价发行。 |
| Under the effective interest method, interest expense equals the carrying amount multiplied by the market rate. | 实际利率法下，利息费用等于账面价值乘以市场利率。 |
| Convertible bonds contain both a liability component and an equity component. | 可转换债券同时包含负债成分与权益成分。 |
| The equity component is measured as the residual amount after deducting the liability component. | 权益成分按发行总额扣除负债成分后的剩余金额计量。 |
| Understating liabilities is a common form of financial statement fraud. | 低估负债是财务报表舞弊的常见形式。 |

### 第 8 章 Owners’ Equity / 所有者权益

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Owners’ equity is the residual interest in the assets of an entity after deducting liabilities. | 所有者权益是主体资产扣除负债后的剩余利益。 |
| Ordinary shares carry voting rights and a claim on residual profits. | 普通股享有表决权与剩余利润分配权。 |
| Share premium is the amount received in excess of the par value of shares. | 股本溢价是发行股份所得超过面值的部分。 |
| Treasury shares are deducted from equity and do not create a gain or loss. | 库藏股冲减权益，不产生损益。 |
| A stock dividend transfers retained earnings to share capital without changing total equity. | 股票股利把留存收益转入股本，权益总额不变。 |
| Dividends are recognised as a liability when declared. | 股利在宣告时确认为负债。 |
| A prior period error is corrected by restating the opening retained earnings. | 前期差错通过重述期初留存收益更正。 |
| Return on equity measures the return generated on shareholders’ investment. | 净资产收益率衡量股东投入所获得的回报。 |

### 第 9 章 Performance / 经营成果

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Revenue is recognised when control of the promised goods or services transfers to the customer. | 当所承诺商品或服务的控制权转移给客户时确认收入。 |
| The transaction price is allocated to each performance obligation on the basis of relative stand-alone selling prices. | 交易价格按各履约义务单独售价的相对比例分摊。 |
| Revenue arises from ordinary activities, whereas gains may arise from incidental transactions. | 收入来自日常活动，利得可能来自偶发交易。 |
| Expenses are matched with the revenues they help to generate. | 费用与它们所帮助产生的收入相配比。 |
| Product costs are inventoried, while period costs are expensed as incurred. | 产品成本计入存货，期间费用在发生时费用化。 |
| Operating profit is profit before interest and tax. | 营业利润是息税前利润。 |
| Basic EPS equals profit attributable to ordinary shareholders divided by the weighted average number of shares. | 基本每股收益等于归属于普通股股东的利润除以加权平均股数。 |
| Revenue recognition fraud often involves recording sales before control transfers. | 收入确认舞弊常常表现为在控制权转移之前记录销售。 |

### 第 10 章 Financial Statement Analysis / 财务报表分析

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| The current ratio measures an entity’s ability to meet short-term obligations. | 流动比率衡量主体偿还短期债务的能力。 |
| Inventory turnover indicates how quickly inventory is sold. | 存货周转率反映存货销售的速度。 |
| A high debt ratio suggests greater financial risk. | 较高的资产负债率意味着更大的财务风险。 |
| Return on equity is decomposed into net profit margin, asset turnover and equity multiplier. | 净资产收益率可分解为净利率、总资产周转率与权益乘数。 |
| Common-size statements express each item as a percentage of a base figure. | 共同比报表把各项目表示为某一基数的百分比。 |
| Analytical procedures are used by auditors to identify unusual relationships. | 审计人员运用分析性程序识别异常关系。 |
| Ratio analysis is limited by differences in accounting policies and industry characteristics. | 比率分析受会计政策差异与行业特征的限制。 |
| Window dressing temporarily improves ratios at the reporting date. | 粉饰报表是在报告日临时改善比率。 |


## Part 3 成本与管理会计

### 第 11 章 An Introduction to Cost Terms and Concepts / 成本术语与概念介绍

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| A cost object is any item for which costs are measured and assigned. | 成本对象是据以计量和分配成本的任何项目。 |
| Direct costs can be traced economically to a cost object. | 直接成本能够经济地追溯到成本对象。 |
| Prime cost is the sum of direct materials and direct labour. | 主要成本是直接材料与直接人工之和。 |
| Product costs are inventoried until the related goods are sold. | 产品成本在相关商品售出前计入存货。 |
| A predetermined overhead rate is budgeted overhead divided by budgeted activity. | 预定制造费用分配率等于预算制造费用除以预算业务量。 |
| Over-applied overhead occurs when applied overhead exceeds actual overhead. | 当已分配制造费用超过实际制造费用时，形成多分配。 |
| Sunk costs are irrelevant to decision making because they cannot be changed. | 沉没成本与决策无关，因为无法改变。 |
| The allocation base should be the cost driver with the strongest causal relationship. | 分配基础应选择因果关系最强的成本动因。 |

### 第 12 章 Costing Systems / 成本核算系统

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Job costing is used when products are manufactured to individual customer orders. | 分批成本法适用于按客户订单生产产品的企业。 |
| Process costing averages costs over equivalent units of production. | 分步成本法按约当产量平均计算成本。 |
| Under absorption costing, fixed manufacturing overhead is included in product cost. | 吸收成本法下，固定制造费用计入产品成本。 |
| Marginal costing treats fixed manufacturing overhead as a period cost. | 变动成本法把固定制造费用视为期间费用。 |
| The difference in profit arises from fixed overhead carried in inventory. | 利润差异来自存货中所含的固定制造费用。 |
| Activity-based costing assigns overhead to products according to the activities they consume. | 作业成本法按产品消耗的作业分配制造费用。 |
| ABC is most useful when overhead is large and products are diverse. | 当制造费用比重大、产品多样时，作业成本法最有价值。 |
| Traditional costing may cross-subsidise high-volume products with low-volume ones. | 传统成本法可能让高产量的产品补贴低产量产品。 |

### 第 13 章 Management Accounting / 管理会计

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| The break-even point is the level of sales at which total revenue equals total cost. | 保本点是总收入等于总成本时的销售水平。 |
| Contribution margin equals sales revenue less variable costs. | 贡献毛益等于销售收入减去变动成本。 |
| The margin of safety measures how much sales can fall before a loss is incurred. | 安全边际衡量销售下降多少才会发生亏损。 |
| A price variance measures the effect of paying more or less than the standard price. | 价格差异衡量实际支付价格与标准价格之差的影响。 |
| An unfavourable variance may require investigation under management by exception. | 在例外管理下，不利差异可能需要调查。 |
| The production budget is derived from the sales budget and the required closing inventory. | 生产预算由销售预算与期末存货需求推出。 |
| Residual income encourages managers to accept projects earning above the cost of capital. | 剩余收益鼓励管理者接受收益高于资本成本的项目。 |
| The balanced scorecard combines financial and non-financial performance measures. | 平衡计分卡把财务与非财务业绩指标结合起来。 |


## Part 4 国际会计

### 第 14 章 International Accounting Harmonization / 国际会计协调

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Harmonization aims to increase the comparability of financial statements across countries. | 协调的目标是提高各国财务报表的可比性。 |
| Harmonization differs from standardization in that it permits some flexibility in accounting treatments. | 协调与标准化的差别在于协调允许一定的会计处理弹性。 |
| Convergence describes the process of gradually eliminating differences between national standards and IFRS. | 趋同描述的是逐步消除本国准则与 IFRS 差异的过程。 |
| From 2005, listed companies in the EU have been required to prepare consolidated statements under IFRS. | 自 2005 年起，欧盟上市公司合并报表被要求采用 IFRS。 |
| IOSCO recommended that securities regulators accept IFRS for cross-border offerings. | IOSCO 建议证券监管机构在跨境发行中接受 IFRS。 |
| IFRS are principles-based and require the exercise of professional judgement. | IFRS 以原则为导向，要求运用职业判断。 |
| Consistent enforcement is essential for the credibility of global standards. | 一致的执行对全球准则的可信度至关重要。 |
| Differences in legal systems and tax regimes remain major obstacles to harmonization. | 法律体系与税制的差异仍是协调的主要障碍。 |

### 第 16 章 Special Accounting Treatments in Multinational Corporations / 跨国公司的特殊业务会计处理

## Part 5 审计

### 第 17 章 Audit Framework / 审计框架

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| The separation of ownership and control creates a demand for independent audit. | 所有权与经营权的分离产生了对独立审计的需求。 |
| An audit provides reasonable, but not absolute, assurance on the financial statements. | 审计对财务报表提供合理保证，而非绝对保证。 |
| The auditor expresses an opinion on whether the financial statements are prepared, in all material respects, in accordance with the applicable framework. | 审计师就财务报表是否在所有重大方面按适用框架编制发表意见。 |
| Internal audit is performed to evaluate internal control and risk management for the entity. | 内部审计旨在评价主体的内部控制与风险管理。 |
| The five fundamental principles are integrity, objectivity, professional competence and due care, confidentiality and professional behaviour. | 五项基本原则是诚信、客观、专业胜任能力与应有的关注、保密与良好职业行为。 |
| Familiarity threat arises when the auditor becomes too sympathetic to the client’s interests. | 密切关系威胁产生于审计师对客户利益过于同情。 |
| Auditors must be independent in mind and in appearance. | 审计师必须做到实质独立与形式独立。 |
| Professional scepticism means maintaining a questioning mind and being alert to contradictory evidence. | 职业怀疑意味着保持质疑的头脑并对矛盾证据保持警觉。 |

### 第 18 章 Collecting Audit Evidence / 收集审计证据

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Audit risk is the risk that the auditor expresses an inappropriate opinion when the financial statements are materially misstated. | 审计风险是指财务报表存在重大错报时审计师发表不恰当意见的风险。 |
| Detection risk is controlled by the auditor through the nature, timing and extent of audit procedures. | 检查风险由审计师通过审计程序的性质、时间与范围加以控制。 |
| Materiality is the amount by which misstatements could influence the economic decisions of users. | 重要性是指错报可能影响使用者经济决策的金额。 |
| COSO internal control consists of five components: control environment, risk assessment, control activities, information and communication, and monitoring activities. | COSO 内部控制由控制环境、风险评估、控制活动、信息与沟通、监控活动五要素构成。 |
| Significant risks require substantive procedures and cannot be addressed by tests of controls alone. | 特别风险必须实施实质性程序，不能仅依赖控制测试。 |
| Inquiry alone is not sufficient to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence. | 仅凭询问不足以获取充分适当的审计证据。 |
| Evidence obtained directly by the auditor is more reliable than evidence obtained indirectly. | 审计师直接获取的证据比间接获得的证据更可靠。 |
| Auditors are required to presume a risk of fraud in revenue recognition. | 审计师须推定收入确认存在舞弊风险。 |
| When a confirmation is not returned, the auditor performs alternative procedures. | 函证未回函时，审计师执行替代程序。 |
| The cut-off test compares shipping documents with sales invoices around the year end. | 截止测试比较年末前后的发货凭证与销售发票。 |
